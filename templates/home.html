The AI-Powered Food Rescue Pipeline

Six specialized autonomous agents work together to ensure food safety, eliminate hoarding, forecast surpluses, and dispatch volunteer couriers.

AGENT 01
Food Analysis Agent

Evaluates food category rules, storage temperatures, preparation timestamps, and allergen declarations. Generates a suitability score (0â€“100) before any food can be published.

AGENT 02
