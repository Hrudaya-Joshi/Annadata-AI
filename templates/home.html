Four-Stage Protocol

How ANNADATA AI Operates

A closed-loop dispatch ecosystem engineered for speed, hygiene compliance, and community dignity.

1. Surplus Logged

Food provider uses our 6-step wizard to register surplus, specify portions, holding temperatures, and expiry limits.

2. Safety & Passport

Deterministic rules screen the donation. A verifiable Food Rescue Passport with unique QR code is generated instantly.

3. 100-Pt Smart Match

The Smart Allocation Engine ranks local NGOs by distance, dietary fit, capacity, active demand, and fair load-balancing.

4. Verified Delivery

Volunteers transport food. NGOs verify handover with a secure PIN. Social and environmental impact is recorded.

Predictive Architecture

AI Intelligence Center

Instead of reacting only when food is already at risk of spoiling, ANNADATA AI uses historical donation patterns and moving averages to predict when and where surplus spikes will occur.

Surplus Demand Forecasting

Detects day-of-week and time-of-day restaurant excess patterns to pre-alert volunteers before kitchen closing.

Fairness Load-Balancing Mechanism

Prevents monopolization of all donations by massive NGOs through an equitable 7-day rolling allocation formula.

Emergency Escalation Protocol

Automated countdown alarms notify backup NGOs and on-call couriers when donations reach the 2-hour deadline window.
