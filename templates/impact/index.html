Scientific Environmental Methodology & Documented Assumptions
CO₂e Landfill Avoidance Ratio: 1.0 kg of edible food rescued avoids approximately 2.5 kg CO₂e of greenhouse gas emissions that would otherwise generate via anaerobic methane decomposition in municipal landfills (United Nations Environment Programme Food Waste Index standard).
Embedded Agricultural Water: 1.0 kg of prepared mixed meals represents an average of 250 Liters of embedded irrigation and agricultural supply chain water (Water Footprint Network standard).
Scientific Integrity Policy: Environmental metrics are calculated as scientifically calibrated estimates. Only successfully delivered donations are accounted for. Pending donations are strictly excluded until receipt is verified by the destination shelter.
