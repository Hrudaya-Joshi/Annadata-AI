Forecasting Methodology & Transparency Architecture
Moving Average Aggregation: Surplus records are grouped into day-of-week and hourly buckets. Exponential moving averages smooth out episodic outliers such as one-time festival cancellations.
Confidence Level Derivation: Confidence scores are directly proportional to the historical sample size (\(N\)), ensuring smaller kitchens are not assigned artificially high statistical confidence.
Simulation Baseline Fallback: If the local database contains fewer than 5 historical batches, the system explicitly labels figures as simulated rather than fabricating false historical accuracy.
